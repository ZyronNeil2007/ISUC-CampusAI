# Sample Admission Requirements and Schedule

This bulletin explains how to apply to Sample University for academic year 2027-2028. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Who may apply

Graduating Grade 12 students and Senior High School graduates may apply for admission as freshmen. Transferees and returning students do not take the admission test. They go to the Registrar and follow the Sample Enrollment Procedure.

## Application period

The application period runs from January 11, 2027 to March 31, 2027. Applications submitted after March 31 are not accepted.

## Application documents

- Accomplished online application form.
- Photocopy of the Grade 11 and Grade 12 report card (Form 138).
- Photocopy of the PSA-issued birth certificate.
- Two 2x2 ID pictures.

## Admission test

Applicants take the Sample University Admission Test (SUAT) at the Testing Center on the Main Campus. A test permit is issued after you apply online. Bring the test permit, a valid ID and two pencils. Mobile phones and calculators are not allowed in the test room.

The SUAT is given on April 3 and April 10, 2027. Results are posted on the admissions portal within three weeks after the test.

## Checking your admission status

Applicants check their admission status on the admissions portal using the application number printed on the test permit. The Admissions Office also posts the list of qualified applicants on its bulletin board.

## After you pass the SUAT

Applicants who pass receive an admission slip. Take the admission slip and your original documents to the Registrar to enroll. Slots in each program are filled in order of test ranking, so enroll as early as you can.

## Appeals

An applicant who was not admitted may appeal once, in writing, to the Director of Admissions within five working days after the results are posted. The appeal must include the test permit and the reason for the appeal.
