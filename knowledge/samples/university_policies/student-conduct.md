# Sample Student Conduct and Discipline

This policy explains the rules of conduct for students and how violations are handled. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## School ID and dress code

Students must wear their school ID at all times inside the campus. Students attend classes in decent attire. Slippers, sleeveless tops and shorts above the knee are not allowed in classrooms and offices.

## Minor offenses

Minor offenses include not wearing the school ID, littering and being noisy near classrooms. The first offense is a written warning. The second offense is a reprimand. The third offense is treated as a major offense.

## Major offenses

Major offenses include cheating, plagiarism, vandalism, possession of prohibited items and disrespect to faculty or staff. Sanctions range from probation to suspension or dismissal, depending on the gravity of the offense.

## Filing a complaint

A complaint against a student is filed in writing with the Office of Student Affairs within 30 days after the incident. The student concerned receives a copy of the complaint and has five working days to submit a written answer. A student with a pending disciplinary case cannot receive a Certificate of Good Moral Character or a scholarship.

## Academic dishonesty

A student caught cheating in an examination receives a grade of 5.00 in the subject and may be suspended. Plagiarism in a thesis or capstone project is a major offense.
