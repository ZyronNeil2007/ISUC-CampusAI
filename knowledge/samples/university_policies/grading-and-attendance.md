# Sample Grading and Attendance Policy

This policy explains grades, absences, dropping subjects, leaves of absence and shifting programs. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Grading system

| Grade | Description | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| 1.00 | Excellent | 98 to 100 |
| 1.25 | Superior | 95 to 97 |
| 1.50 | Very good | 92 to 94 |
| 1.75 | Good | 89 to 91 |
| 2.00 | Satisfactory | 86 to 88 |
| 2.25 | Fairly satisfactory | 83 to 85 |
| 2.50 | Fair | 80 to 82 |
| 2.75 | Passing | 77 to 79 |
| 3.00 | Passing | 75 to 76 |
| 5.00 | Failed | Below 75 |
| INC | Incomplete | Not applicable |
| DRP | Dropped | Not applicable |

The lowest passing grade is 3.00. A student who gets 5.00 in a subject must take the subject again.

## Incomplete grade

A student with a grade of INC must complete the missing requirements within one academic year. If the requirements are not completed by then, the INC becomes 5.00.

## Failing a subject

A student who fails a subject must enroll in it again the next time it is offered. A student may not take the same subject more than three times without the approval of the Dean.

## Attendance

A student who is absent for more than 20 percent of the class hours of a subject may be dropped from the subject by the instructor. An absence is excused when the student submits an excuse letter within three days after returning. A medical certificate is needed for absences caused by illness of more than two days.

## Dropping subjects

A subject dropped within the first two weeks of classes leaves no grade on the student's record. A subject dropped after the first two weeks but before the midterm examination is recorded as DRP. A subject dropped after the midterm examination without the Dean's approval is graded 5.00.

## Leave of absence

A student may file a leave of absence for up to two consecutive semesters. The student applies to the Dean and the Registrar before the midterm examination of the semester. A student who does not return after the approved leave is considered withdrawn.

## Shifting programs

A student may shift to another program once. To shift, the student must have completed at least one semester, have a GWA of 2.25 or better, and have the approval of both the current Dean and the receiving Dean. Shifting requests are filed at the Registrar before the end of the second week of the semester.
