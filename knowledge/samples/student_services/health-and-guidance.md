# Sample Health and Guidance Services

This page describes the clinic and counseling services for students. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## University Clinic

The University Clinic gives first aid and basic medical consultations. It is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Students who need hospital care are referred to the nearest hospital. The clinic does not dispense prescription medicine.

## Medical clearance

New students need a medical clearance from the University Clinic for enrollment. The clearance is free. Bring your admission slip and a valid ID to the clinic.

## Guidance and counseling

The Guidance and Counseling Center offers free and confidential counseling for personal, academic and career concerns. Students may book an appointment at the Guidance office or by email. Walk-in students are accepted when a counselor is free.

Counseling records are confidential. They are shared only when a student may be in danger or when the law requires it.

## Reporting harassment

A student who experiences or sees harassment may report it to the Guidance and Counseling Center or to the Office of Student Affairs. Reports are handled confidentially, and the student who reports is protected from retaliation.
