# Sample Campus Wi-Fi and Student ID Guide

This guide explains how to get campus Wi-Fi and how to replace a lost student ID. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Campus Wi-Fi vouchers

Enrolled students get free campus Wi-Fi through vouchers issued by the Information Technology Office. A voucher gives 5 hours of access per day and is valid for 30 days. A voucher works on one device at a time.

To get a voucher, go to the Information Technology Office and show your Certificate of Registration (COR) and your school ID. Student portal passwords are also reset at this office.

## Replacing a lost student ID

1. Report the loss to the Office of Student Affairs and get a replacement slip.
2. Pay the replacement fee of 150 pesos at the Cashier and keep the official receipt.
3. Submit the replacement slip, the official receipt and one 1x1 ID picture to the Office of Student Affairs.
4. Claim the new ID after 5 working days.

## ID validation

The student ID is validated every semester. Bring your Certificate of Registration to the Office of Student Affairs after you enroll to get the validation sticker.
