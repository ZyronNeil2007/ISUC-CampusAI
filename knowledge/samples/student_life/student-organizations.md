# Sample Student Organizations Guide

This guide explains how student organizations are accredited and how they hold activities. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Accrediting an organization

A new organization applies to the Office of Student Affairs before September 30. The application needs:

- A letter of intent.
- A list of at least 20 members who are enrolled students.
- The constitution and by-laws.
- The written consent of a faculty adviser.
- A list of planned activities for the year.

Accreditation is valid for one academic year, and the organization renews it every year.

## Officers

Officers must be enrolled students with no failing grade and no pending disciplinary case. The president and treasurer must have a GWA of 2.50 or better.

## Holding an activity

At least 10 working days before an activity, the organization submits an activity proposal with a budget to the adviser and the Office of Student Affairs. Activities outside the campus also need signed parental consent forms from every participant. An activity may not start until the Office of Student Affairs approves the proposal in writing.
