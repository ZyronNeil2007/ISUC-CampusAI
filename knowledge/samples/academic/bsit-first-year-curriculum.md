# Sample BS Information Technology Curriculum

This page lists the first-year subjects of the fictional Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program of the Sample College of Computing, and its main requirements. It is sample content used to test CampusAI.

| Code | Subject | Units | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT 101 | Introduction to Computing | 3 | None |
| IT 102 | Computer Programming 1 | 3 | None |
| MATH 101 | Discrete Mathematics | 3 | None |
| GE 101 | Understanding the Self | 3 | None |
| GE 102 | Purposive Communication | 3 | None |
| PE 101 | Physical Fitness | 2 | None |
| NSTP 101 | National Service Training Program 1 | 3 | None |

| PE 102 | Rhythmic Activities | 2 | PE 101 |

A student may enroll in a subject only after passing its prerequisite.

## On-the-job training

Students take the on-the-job training (OJT) of 486 hours in the summer after the third year. To qualify, a student must have passed all IT subjects up to the third year. The student also needs a signed training agreement with the host company.

## Capstone project

In the fourth year students take Capstone Project 1 (IT 401) and Capstone Project 2 (IT 402) in teams. The Dean assigns an adviser to each team. The capstone ends with a final defense before a panel of three faculty members at the end of IT 402.
