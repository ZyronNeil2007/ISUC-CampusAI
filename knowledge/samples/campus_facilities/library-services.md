# Sample University Library Services

This handbook explains how students use the library. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Opening hours

The library is open Monday to Friday from 7:30 AM to 7:00 PM and on Saturday from 8:00 AM to 12:00 noon. It is closed on Sundays and holidays. During final examination week the library stays open until 9:00 PM on weekdays.

## Borrowing books

Enrolled students may borrow books by presenting a valid school ID. The Circulation Section lends up to three books at a time for seven days. A loan may be renewed once for seven more days if nobody else has reserved the book.

Reference books and reserve books are for use inside the library. Reserve books may be borrowed overnight starting at 4:30 PM and must be returned by 8:30 AM the next day.

## Overdue fines

The fine for an overdue circulation book is 5 pesos per day for each book. The fine for an overdue reserve book is 20 pesos per hour. Fines are paid at the Cashier, and unpaid fines must be settled before the library signs your clearance.

## Lost or damaged books

A student who loses or damages a book must replace it with the same title and edition, or pay the current price of the book plus a processing fee of 100 pesos.

## Online Reference Desk

The Online Reference Desk answers research questions by email at library@sample-university.test. Students can also use the e-library on the library computers or from home with their student portal account.
