# Sample Scholarship Programs

Sample University offers three scholarship programs. This is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Programs

### Dean's Merit Grant

The grant covers 100 percent of tuition for the semester. The student must have a general weighted average (GWA) of 1.50 or better in the previous semester, no grade lower than 2.50, and a load of at least 15 units.

### Community Service Scholarship

The scholarship gives a 50 percent tuition discount. The student must have a GWA of 2.25 or better and render 80 hours of community service during the semester.

### Student Assistantship

Student assistants work in a university office for four hours a day and receive an allowance of 2,500 pesos a month. The student must have a GWA of 2.50 or better and no class conflict with the assigned work schedule. Applicants are interviewed by the Scholarship Office.

## Eligibility for all programs

- The applicant is a bona fide enrolled student with a regular load of at least 15 units.
- The applicant has no failing, incomplete or dropped grade in the previous semester.
- The applicant has no pending disciplinary case.

## How to apply

1. Get an application form from the Scholarship Office.
2. Attach a copy of your grades from the previous semester and your Certificate of Registration (COR).
3. Submit the form to the Scholarship Office on or before the deadline.
4. Student Assistantship applicants wait for the interview schedule posted at the Scholarship Office.

## Deadlines

Applications for the second semester are due on January 22, 2027. The deadline for the first semester was August 14, 2026. Late applications are not accepted.

## Renewal

Scholars must apply again every semester. A scholar whose GWA falls below the required level loses the scholarship in the next semester and may apply again once the requirement is met.
