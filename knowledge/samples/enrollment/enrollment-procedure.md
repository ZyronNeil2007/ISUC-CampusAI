# Sample Enrollment Procedure

This guide explains how students enroll at Sample University each semester. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Who enrolls

Every student who wants to take classes in a semester must enroll during the official enrollment period. Continuing students enroll first, followed by transferees, returning students and new freshmen.

## Steps for continuing students

1. Get your clearance from the Library, the Cashier and your college office. A student with an unpaid balance or an unreturned library book cannot be cleared.
2. Meet your academic adviser and have your subjects for the semester approved.
3. Submit the approved subject list to the Registrar for the assessment of fees.
4. Pay the assessed amount at the Cashier and keep the official receipt.
5. Present the official receipt to the Registrar to receive your Certificate of Registration (COR).

Enrollment is complete only when you have received your COR.

## Requirements for new freshmen

- Admission slip showing that you passed the Sample University Admission Test (SUAT).
- Original Form 138 (report card).
- Certificate of Good Moral Character from the last school attended.
- Photocopy of the PSA-issued birth certificate.
- Two 2x2 ID pictures with a white background.
- Medical clearance from the University Clinic.

## Requirements for transferees

- Transcript of Records (TOR) marked "For Transfer".
- Honorable Dismissal.
- Certificate of Good Moral Character.
- Photocopy of the PSA-issued birth certificate.
- Evaluation of credits by the college office before subjects are assigned.

## Returning students

A student who is coming back from an approved leave of absence presents the approved leave form to the Registrar before enrolling, then follows the steps for continuing students.

## Late enrollment

Late enrollment is accepted until the tenth school day after classes begin. A late enrollment fee of 300 pesos is added to the assessed fees. Students who enroll late are responsible for the lessons they missed. After the tenth school day enrollment is closed for the semester, unless the Dean gives written approval.

## Adding and dropping subjects

A student may add or drop subjects only during the first two weeks of classes. Dropping a subject after that period is recorded as explained in the Sample Grading and Attendance Policy.
