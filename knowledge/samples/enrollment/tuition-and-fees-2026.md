# Sample Tuition and Fees, 2026-2027

These fees apply to academic year 2026-2027. This is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## Tuition

Tuition is 1,200 pesos per unit. A student with a regular load of 21 units pays 25,200 pesos in tuition for the semester.

## Other school fees

The following fees are charged every semester.

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Registration fee | 150 pesos |
| Library fee | 300 pesos |
| Medical and dental fee | 250 pesos |
| Student activity fee | 200 pesos |
| Laboratory fee, per laboratory subject | 600 pesos |

## How to pay

Fees are paid at the Cashier after the Registrar has assessed them. The Cashier accepts cash. Keep the official receipt, because the Registrar needs it to release your Certificate of Registration.

## Installment payment

A student may pay in two installments. At least 50 percent of the total assessed amount is paid at enrollment, and the balance is paid before the midterm examination. A student with an unpaid balance cannot take the final examinations or receive clearance.

## Refunds

A student who withdraws from all subjects within the first week of classes is refunded 80 percent of the tuition. No refund is given after the first week.
