# Sample Guide to Requesting Documents

This guide explains how to request academic documents from the Registrar. It is fictional sample content used to test CampusAI.

## How to request a document

1. Fill out the Document Request Form at the Registrar's counter.
2. Pay the fee at the Cashier and keep the official receipt.
3. Submit the form and the official receipt at the Registrar's counter and get a claim stub.
4. Claim the document on the date written on the claim stub. Bring the claim stub and a valid ID.

## Documents, fees and processing time

| Document | Fee | Processing time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transcript of Records (TOR) | 150 pesos per copy | 7 working days | Requires clearance |
| Certificate of Enrollment | 50 pesos | 1 working day | |
| Certificate of Grades | 50 pesos | 2 working days | |
| Certificate of Good Moral Character | 75 pesos | 3 working days | Requires clearance from the Office of Student Affairs |
| Certified True Copy of Diploma | 100 pesos | 5 working days | |

## Rush requests

A rush request for a Transcript of Records is released in 2 working days instead of 7. The rush fee is 100 pesos in addition to the regular fee. Rush processing is available only for the Transcript of Records.

## Claiming through a representative

If you cannot claim the document yourself, your representative must bring all of the following:

- An authorization letter signed by you.
- A photocopy of your valid ID showing your signature.
- The claim stub.
- The representative's own valid ID.

## Clearance

A student with an unpaid balance, an unreturned library book or a pending disciplinary case cannot be issued a Transcript of Records or a Certificate of Good Moral Character until the matter is cleared.
